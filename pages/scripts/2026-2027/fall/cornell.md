## Pregame

Ladies and gentlemen, friends and alumni, presenting an organization that is exceeding its curfew, it’s the Brown University, “please don’t get mad at me Mommy” BANDDDD!

Let’s set the scene. Six o’clock in the morning. You’re asleep in your dorm awaiting a quiet breakfast with your parents, but there’s one problem. Unbeknownst to you, the orange juice that you spilled on your alarm clock last night has completely broken the thing. You’re going to oversleep and miss your parents, the effects of which will lead to your eventual disownment several years down the line… unless, by some miracle, the Brown University Band stands outside your dorm and blasts “Uptown Funk” directly into your first-floor window. Congratulations, your precious inheritance has been saved! No need to thank us.

We woke up at 8 in the morning today out of our volition. Our grooming habits are impeccable—peep the uniform. We read through every single email that hits our inboxes, thanks, Shun! Contact us by email, phone, or fax if you’re ready for 40-odd additions to your household.
